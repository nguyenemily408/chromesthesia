Chromesthesia

Play a song and watch it paint itself. Each of the 12 notes has its own color. Loud, bright sounds glow neon; quiet, low ones stay dull.

YouTube or Spotify: play the song in another Chrome tab, choose Listen to a browser tab, pick that tab and tick Also share tab audio. You can also drop a music file anywhere on the painting. Keys 1 to 9 switch styles.
